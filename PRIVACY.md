# Privacy Policy for BlobBergs

**Effective date:** May 21, 2026  
**Last updated:** May 21, 2026

Mulcahy Heavy Industries (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the BlobBergs mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it on iPhone and iPad.

We wrote this policy to match what the App actually does today: it is a local, offline arcade game with no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

---

## Summary

- **We do not collect personal information from you** and do not operate servers that receive your data.
- **The App does not require an internet connection** to play.
- **Gameplay data stays on your device** (for example, your high score and Zen Mode setting).
- **We do not sell your data**, use it for cross-app tracking, or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

---

## What BlobBergs Is

BlobBergs is a casual game in which you launch a blob between drifting ice floes. The App uses touch input in real time to run the game. It does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or other contact details.

---

## Information the App Uses (On Your Device Only)

### Gameplay and touch input

While you play, the App processes touch gestures and timing on your device to control launches, orbiting, and collisions. This input is used immediately for gameplay and is **not stored** as a history of your touches or sessions.

### Data stored locally on your device

The App saves a small amount of information on your device using Apple’s standard on-device storage (`UserDefaults`), so your preferences and progress persist between launches:

| What is stored | Purpose | Stored where |
|----------------|---------|--------------|
| High score | Show your best score and track new records | On your device only |
| Zen Mode on/off | Remember whether you enabled relaxed play (no scoring or game-over) | On your device only |

Other settings you can adjust in the in-app settings panel (such as launch speed or floe count) apply while the App is running. **Only Zen Mode is saved** between app launches; other tuning values return to built-in defaults when you restart the App unless you change them again in the current session.

We do **not** transmit this locally stored information to our servers or to third parties, because the App has no network features for sending that data.

### What we do not access

The App does **not** request or use:

- Location services  
- Camera or photo library  
- Microphone  
- Contacts, calendars, or reminders  
- Health data  
- Apple ID or other sign-in credentials  
- Advertising identifiers for tracking  
- Push notifications  

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

Because the App works entirely on your device and does not connect to our backend services, we do **not** collect:

- Name, email address, phone number, or mailing address  
- Account credentials  
- Payment or purchase history (the App does not include in-app purchases in its current version)  
- Browsing or search history outside the App  
- Device identifiers sent to us for profiling or marketing  
- Analytics or crash reports sent to us or third-party analytics providers  

If we add features in the future that collect or transmit information, we will update this policy before or when those features ship, and we will update App Store privacy disclosures as required.

## Legal Bases and Your Rights (Where Applicable)

### United States (including California)

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are commonly used in U.S. state privacy laws, because we do not collect personal information off your device. California residents may contact us with privacy questions; we will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

### European Economic Area, UK, and similar regions

If privacy laws in your region apply, our role is limited: we do not process personal data on our systems. Data that exists only on your device is under your control. You may delete locally stored App data by removing the App from your device or resetting the App’s data through iOS settings where available.

### Requests

Because we do not operate a user database tied to the App, we generally cannot access, correct, export, or delete data stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App removes its local storage from your device under normal iOS behavior.

## Data Security and Retention

Data stored by the App remains on your device and is protected by your device’s passcode, biometrics, and iOS security features. We do not retain copies on our servers because we do not receive that data.

Your high score and Zen Mode setting remain on the device until you delete the App, clear the App’s data, or replace your device without restoring from a backup that included the App.

---

## International Users

The App is offered for download through the Apple App Store. If you use the App outside the United States, your on-device data is still processed locally on your device; we do not transfer it to our servers.

## App Store Privacy Labels (Disclosure Helper)

When completing Apple’s App Privacy questionnaire for the current version of BlobBergs, the accurate characterization is typically:

- **Data Collection:** Data Not Collected (no data transmitted off the device in a way that you or partners can access later).

- **Linked to You / Used for Tracking:** Not applicable for data sent to us, because we do not receive it.

On-device high score and settings are not “collected” in Apple’s sense unless they leave the device; this App does not send them off the device.

Always confirm answers against the latest [Apple App Privacy Details documentation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/) when submitting each build.
